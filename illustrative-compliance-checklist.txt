ILLUSTRATIVE COMPLIANCE CHECKLIST (demo purposes only, not real firm policy):

1. Any mention of past performance must include: "Past performance is not indicative of future results."
2. Any specific return figure must be paired with an "as of [date]" label.
3. Prohibited guarantee language: "guaranteed," "risk-free," "no risk," "best," "top-performing" (unless sourced and qualified).
4. Benchmark comparisons must name the benchmark and time period.
5. Any specific fund mention requires an applicable risk disclosure.
6. Retail-facing materials must avoid unexplained technical jargon.
7. Social posts citing performance figures need an abbreviated disclaimer plus a link to full disclosures.
8. No forward-looking performance claims ("will outperform," "expected to deliver X%").
9. Third-party testimonials always require elevated human review.
10. Institutional-only content shared publicly requires a predistribution-restriction check.
11. Fee/expense figures must not imply misleading net-of-fee returns.
12. Third-party rankings/awards require a date and named source.
13. Any competitor comparison always requires compliance review, regardless of other flags.
